Privacy policy

Updated Nov 22, 2026

Browsing the website

Dark Echo does not require an account or collect messages through a form hosted on this website. The hosting provider may process technical request information needed to serve pages. Search and filters run in your browser; their state may be included in the page URL so you can share the same results.

Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics loads only after you choose “Allow analytics,” or if your browser remembers an earlier agreement. Choosing “Reject analytics” lets you browse without loading the Analytics tag.

When allowed, Analytics can receive page, browser, device, approximate location, and engagement information. We also measure actions such as opening a video, following a YouTube link, or moving from a story to a video. Advertising storage and personalization remain disabled in this site’s configuration.

Remembering and changing your choice

Your choice is stored in browser local storage. If Analytics is enabled, it may use cookies. You can change the choice using the button below or “Analytics choices” in the footer. Changing from allow to reject disables measurement, clears known Analytics cookies for this site, and reloads the page.

YouTube playback

Collection pages use locally hosted thumbnail images. Their YouTube player loads when you choose quick play. Individual watch pages include a visible YouTube player as the main content of the page.

We use YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player. Viewing or interacting with embedded videos may still involve Google or YouTube processing information under their own policies. The Analytics choice controls this website’s optional Analytics tag; it does not control YouTube playback or its separate processing.

External links and feedback

Following a YouTube or GitHub link takes you to that provider. The website feedback form is hosted on GitHub, requires an account, and creates a public report when you submit it. We do not submit that form on your behalf.

Questions and updates

Use the contact page for questions about the site or this policy. This page will be updated when the site’s services or choices change.
